## Weekly PM Review Workflow

1. Open `pm-workspace/@tasks/active.md` and list what was completed, in progress, and not started
   - Review last week's planned items
   - Mark what was actually done
   - Identify what carried over
   - Note any surprises or unexpected work

2. Open `pm-workspace/@knowledge/CLAUDE.md` and check if strategy, metrics, or personas need updating
   - Did you learn anything that shifts your understanding of users?
   - Did any metrics prove to be wrong proxies?
   - Did strategy need adjustment based on market or regulatory signals?
   - Propose updates if needed, ask before applying

3. Suggest 3-5 priorities for next week based on `pm-workspace/GOALS.md`
   - Map to quarterly OKRs
   - Consider dependencies and blockers
   - Balance work across HeartGuide, GlucoWise, BreathEasy, DocuFlow
   - Prioritize using RICE or MoSCoW if helpful

4. Update `pm-workspace/@tasks/active.md` with next week's focus items
   - Write the new top 3
   - Move incomplete items (decide: carry over or drop?)
   - Add any new items from ideation
   - Update the "Date Updated" field

5. Output a one-paragraph summary I can paste into a Notion or Slack update
   - Template: "This week I [shipped X], learned [Y], and [decided Z / got blocked by W]. Next week I'm focused on [top priority]."
   - Keep it honest—include blockers and failures, not just wins
