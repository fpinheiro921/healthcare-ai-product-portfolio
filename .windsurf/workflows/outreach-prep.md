## Company Outreach Prep Workflow

1. Ask me: "Which company and role are you preparing for?"
   - Capture company name and specific role (if known)
   - Ask about context: first outreach, interview prep, or follow-up?
   - Ask about my current knowledge level of the company

2. Search for recent news, product launches, and strategy signals for that company
   - Recent press releases or announcements (last 6-12 months)
   - Product launches or feature updates
   - Leadership changes or key hires
   - Funding rounds or financial signals
   - Regulatory milestones (FDA clearances, CE marks)
   - Clinical validation studies or outcomes data
   - Partnership announcements (health systems, payers)
   - Any controversies or challenges they're facing

3. Map their product challenges to my clinical + product background
   - Identify their target clinical domain (cardiometabolic, MSK, etc.)
   - Map to my experience: "You treated patients with [condition]"
   - Map to my portfolio: HeartGuide (HF), GlucoWise (diabetes), BreathEasy (respiratory), DocuFlow (documentation)
   - Identify gaps I could fill: "They're expanding to [area] where you have [experience]"
   - Note any regulatory or clinical validation challenges they've faced

4. Draft 3 talking points that position me as a clinical product builder
   - Talking point 1: Clinical credibility angle ("I've treated patients with...")
   - Talking point 2: Product thinking angle ("I built prototypes that...")
   - Talking point 3: Domain expertise angle ("The regulatory challenge you're facing...")
   - Each should tie my background to their specific needs/challenges
   - Avoid generic statements—make them specific to the company

5. Suggest 2-3 smart questions I could ask them
   - Question 1: Strategy/deep thinking (shows I understand their business)
   - Question 2: Clinical validation/outcomes (shows I care about evidence)
   - Question 3: Role-specific (shows I'm serious about the opportunity)
   - Questions should demonstrate expertise, not just curiosity
   - Avoid questions easily answered by their website
