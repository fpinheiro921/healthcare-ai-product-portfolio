# Clinical Product Rules

## Domain Context
- This workspace contains AI clinical app concepts and a PM Operating System
- Main apps: HeartGuide AI, GlucoWise AI, BreathEasy AI, DocuFlow
- Apps location: project-3-mvps/{heartguide-ai, glucowise-ai, breatheasy-ai, docuflow}/
- Portfolio website: portfolio-website/
- PM OS: pm-workspace/

## Clinical Accuracy
- HeartGuide targets heart failure — never confuse with general cardiology
- GlucoWise targets Type 2 diabetes on insulin — NEVER reference Type 1 diabetes
- BreathEasy targets asthma AND COPD — always include both conditions
- DocuFlow targets clinical documentation workflow — not general document management
- All clinical claims must cite sources (journal, year, sample size)
- Distinguish between validated data and projections/estimates

## Regulatory Awareness
- FDA SaMD and MDR are always relevant context
- Never skip regulatory considerations in product discussions
- 510(k), De Novo, and CE Marking pathways should be referenced where appropriate
- HIPAA and GDPR compliance are baseline requirements, not features

## Product Thinking
- Problem-first, not solution-first
- Clinical validation trumps user engagement
- B2B2C (health systems/insurers) over DTC for chronic care
- Outcomes (HbA1c, readmissions, exacerbations) over engagement metrics (DAU)
- Clinician trust is harder to earn than patient trust — prioritize accordingly
