# Weekly PM Review Ritual

## Purpose
Structured reflection on progress, learning, and priorities. 30 minutes every Friday or Monday morning.

---

## Step 1 — Review Last Week (5 min)

**Prompts:**
1. What did I ship? (Even small wins count)
2. What did I learn? (Clinical, product, or technical)
3. What blocked me? (Dependencies, unknowns, energy)
4. What would I do differently?

**Output format:**
- ✅ Shipped: [3-5 items]
- 📚 Learned: [2-3 insights]
- 🚧 Blocked by: [1-2 items]
- 🔁 Do differently: [1 reflection]

---

## Step 2 — Check Strategy Alignment (5 min)

**Prompts:**
1. Did last week's work move me toward quarterly OKRs?
2. Did anything shift in my understanding of the problem space?
3. Do my personas still feel accurate?
4. Did any app (HeartGuide, GlucoWise, BreathEasy, DocuFlow) get neglected?

**Output format:**
- OKR alignment score: [0-10]
- Strategic insight: [if any]
- Persona update needed: [yes/no, what changed]
- App balance: [which apps got attention, which didn't]

---

## Step 3 — Prioritize Next Week (10 min)

**Prompts:**
1. What's the ONE thing that would make next week a success?
2. What are 2-3 other important items?
3. What's at risk of falling through the cracks?
4. What meetings/commitments are fixed?

**Output format:**
- 🎯 North Star: [the one thing]
- 📋 Top 3: [ranked by impact]
- ⚠️ At risk: [items to monitor]
- 📅 Fixed commitments: [calendar blocks]

---

## Step 4 — Update Active Tasks (5 min)

**Action:**
Update `pm-workspace/@tasks/active.md` with:
- Last week's actuals (what really happened)
- Next week's plan (top 3-5 items)
- Any decisions needed

---

## Step 5 — Communication Summary (5 min)

**Output a paragraph for:**
- Personal productivity system (Notion, Obsidian)
- Accountability partner, mentor, or community
- Potential employer updates (if relevant)

**Template:**
"This week I [shipped X], learned [Y about clinical workflows/product management/regulatory], and [decided Z / got blocked by W]. Next week I'm focused on [top priority]."

---

## Weekly Review Checklist

- [ ] Reviewed last week's actuals
- [ ] Checked strategy alignment
- [ ] Set next week's top 3
- [ ] Updated active.md
- [ ] Wrote summary paragraph
- [ ] Scheduled next review
